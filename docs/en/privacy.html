Privacy policy

Chrome extension “QA Bug Recorder”. Effective from November 22, 2026.

In short

The extension helps test websites. Everything it collects is stored only in your browser. The extension has no server of its own, shows no ads, and does not sell or share data with third parties. Data leaves the browser only when you do it yourself: download or copy a report, or press the button of a service you connected.

What data is collected

On tabs with websites, after you accepted this policy on the extension welcome page:

  • actions, requests and console, as well as a page replay (changes of its content) and screenshots of the visible part after actions — only for the last 10 minutes, stored in the browser and erased when the tab is closed; replay and screenshots can be turned off in the settings. Tab video and microphone voice — only on your command;
  • actions on the page: clicks, typing in fields, navigation. Values of password, bank card, CVV and token fields are not recorded;
  • technical page data: network requests and responses, console messages, performance metrics, cookies and site storage data, environment details (browser, window size);
  • your comments, marks and the chosen severity.

In addition, the extension stores what you create yourself: autotest scenarios, response mocking rules, API test projects and their results, settings.

Where data is stored

In the browser's local storage on your computer (IndexedDB and extension storage). You can delete any report in “My reports”, or all data by removing the extension.

When data is transferred

  • Export and copying. You decide who receives a downloaded or copied report. Tokens and passwords can be hidden before export.
  • AI analysis (optional). If you entered your own API key of a service (Anthropic, OpenAI or a compatible one), then when you press the 🤖 button the text of the item or report and, if enabled, the screenshot are sent to that service. From then on the privacy policy of the chosen service applies. The key is stored only in your browser.
  • Google Docs (optional). If you connected Google, the report is uploaded on your command to the “QA Bug Recorder” folder on your Google Drive. The extension only gets access to files it created itself.
  • API tests and autotests. Requests go only to the addresses you specified.

Extension permissions

  • Site access — to record requests, the console and actions on the page under test and to show the marks panel.
  • Tab capture and microphone — to record video and voice comments on your command.
  • Storage — so that reports are saved in the browser.
  • Tab debugging (optional, requested on first use) — to emulate a slow network and devices, take screenshots at different screen sizes and capture the full network.
  • Google sign-in — only if you connect Google Docs.

Children

The extension is intended for testing and development professionals and is not intended for children.

Changes

If the policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the date.

Contacts
